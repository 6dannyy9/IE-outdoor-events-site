The easiest way to book is directly with us — send an email to ie.outdoorevents@gmail.com, or call/text Maria at (562) 413-1124 or Victor at (909) 676-5383. Booking direct gives you the best rate. We're happy to walk you through availability and options.

The minimum booking is 4 hours. Weekday rates (Mon–Thu) are $149.99/hour, and weekend rates (Fri–Sun) are $194.99/hour. On weekends, booking 6 or more hours saves $15/hour ($179.99/hour). You can extend your booking as needed — just let us know in advance so we can confirm availability.

Guests are still expected to remove their personal belongings and leave the venue in a generally tidy condition — additional charges may apply for excessive cleaning or damage beyond normal use.

We're open 7 days a week, 6 AM – 10 PM every day. Weekday rates are lower, making midweek events a wonderful value.

Yes, alcoholic beverages are permitted. You're welcome to bring your own bartender or beverage service. We have a full-size refrigerator and two large coolers available to keep everything cold.

Standard 90-day policy: full refund if cancelled 90+ days before the event. 50% refund (excluding fees) if cancelled 14–90 days out. No refund within 14 days. Bookings cancelled within 24 hours of confirmation but at least 48 hours before the event receive a full refund (Grace Period policy).
