A Private Outdoor
Space in the I.E.

Hosted by Victor & Maria T., I.E Outdoor Events is a beautifully maintained private venue in Rialto designed to make your event feel intimate, elegant, and completely yours. No cookie-cutter ballrooms — just an expansive outdoor space with character, warmth, and everything you need built in.

Whether you're planning an intimate baby shower or a full wedding reception with 150 guests, our space flexes to fit your vision — now open seven days a week, with lower rates Monday through Thursday.
